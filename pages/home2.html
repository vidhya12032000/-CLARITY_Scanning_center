EXPLORE OUR TECHNOLOGY

Different scans. One clear purpose.

Each imaging technology answers different clinical questions. Explore how our modalities work and what each one can help your care team understand.

MRI · FIELD ACTIVE
01 / MAGNETIC RESONANCE

Detailed views without radiation.

MRI uses powerful magnetic fields and radio waves to create highly detailed images of soft tissues, organs, joints and the nervous system.

Best for
Brain, spine, joints & soft tissue
Typical time
30–60 minutes
Radiation
No ionizing radiation
Comfort
Noise protection provided
Explore MRI
02 / COMPUTED TOMOGRAPHY

Fast imaging when clarity matters.

CT combines multiple X-ray images with computer processing to create detailed cross-sectional views of the body.

Best for
Chest, abdomen, bones & trauma
Typical time
10–20 minutes
Strength
Fast cross-sectional imaging
Support
Technologist-guided procedure
Explore CT
03 / ULTRASOUND

Real-time imaging, thoughtfully delivered.

Ultrasound uses sound waves to create real-time images and is widely used for abdominal, vascular, thyroid and pregnancy imaging.

Best for
Organs, vessels & soft tissue
Typical time
20–45 minutes
Radiation
No ionizing radiation
Imaging
Real-time examination
Explore Ultrasound
