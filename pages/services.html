FIND YOUR MODALITY

Three technologies. Different strengths.

MRI, CT and ultrasound each provide a different view of the body. Your referring clinician determines which examination is appropriate based on your symptoms and clinical needs.

MRI

Excellent soft-tissue contrast

MRI is particularly useful when detailed soft-tissue information is important.

TECHNOLOGY Magnetic field
STRENGTH Soft tissue detail
COMMON AREAS Brain · Spine · Joints
CT SCAN

Fast cross-sectional imaging

CT can provide detailed cross-sectional views quickly and is widely used for many diagnostic situations.

TECHNOLOGY X-ray based
STRENGTH Speed & detail
COMMON AREAS Chest · Abdomen · Head
ULTRASOUND

Real-time sound-wave imaging

Ultrasound provides dynamic imaging and can be useful for organs, soft tissues and blood-flow assessment.

TECHNOLOGY Sound waves
STRENGTH Real-time imaging
COMMON AREAS Abdomen · Soft tissue
