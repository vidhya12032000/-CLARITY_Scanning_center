Frequently Asked Questions

Questions before your visit?

Here are answers to some common questions about our imaging center, appointments and examinations.

Talk to Our Team

Most examinations are scheduled in advance so we can prepare the appropriate imaging room, protocol and clinical requirements. Contact our team to check appointment availability.

Preparation depends on the examination. Some scans may require fasting, specific clothing or other instructions. Our team will explain the preparation requirements when your appointment is scheduled.

In many cases a companion can accompany you during the registration and waiting process. Access to examination rooms may depend on safety and clinical requirements.

Reporting time varies according to the examination and clinical requirements. Our team will provide the expected reporting timeline for your appointment.

Please bring your referral or prescription, previous relevant imaging reports or scans, identification and any other documents requested during scheduling.
