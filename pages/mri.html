WHY MRI

Detailed imaging when details matter.

Magnetic resonance imaging creates detailed images of structures inside the body by using a strong magnetic field and radio waves.

MRI can be particularly useful for evaluating the brain, spine, joints, muscles, ligaments, organs and other soft tissues.
