MRI Imaging

A large magnet and radio waves build highly detailed pictures of the brain, spine, joints and soft tissue.

How it works
Strong magnet and radio waves
Typical time
About 20 – 60 minutes
Radiation
None
Good to know
Remove all metal. The scanner makes loud knocking sounds, so ear protection is normal.

Typical times only. Your confirmation will include exact timing and preparation.

Explore MRI

CT Scan

A rotating X-ray beam captures cross-sectional slices that a computer assembles into a detailed view of the body.

How it works
Rotating X-ray beam and detectors
Typical time
The scan itself takes minutes; plan for 15 – 30 including preparation
Radiation
Uses a controlled dose of X-rays
Good to know
Some scans use contrast dye. Tell us about allergies and kidney conditions beforehand.

Typical times only. Your confirmation will include exact timing and preparation.

Explore CT

Ultrasound

High-frequency sound waves create live images, which makes it a gentle option for abdominal, vascular and obstetric checks.

How it works
High-frequency sound waves
Typical time
About 15 – 45 minutes
Radiation
None
Good to know
Some exams need fasting or a full bladder. We will tell you before your visit.

Typical times only. Your confirmation will include exact timing and preparation.
